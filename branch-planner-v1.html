How the projection works

  1. Average daily sales = running gross sales ÷ completed operating days.
  2. Month-end sales = average daily sales × planned monthly operating days.
  3. Formula staff = average daily sales ÷ sales divisor, rounded up.
  4. Monthly labor = planned staff × daily salary × paid days + other monthly labor costs.
  5. Labor % = total monthly labor ÷ total projected monthly sales × 100.

Read the KPI correctly

The overall percentage uses combined labor costs divided by combined sales; it is not the average of branch percentages.

The combined KPI budget adds each branch’s individual labor budget. Its percentage is weighted by projected sales. Paid days and operating days can differ. Add estimated employer contributions, benefits, overtime and premiums under “Other monthly labor costs” to include them in the projection.

Staff recommendations are a sales-based planning guide. Review shift coverage, workload and rest days before deployment.
