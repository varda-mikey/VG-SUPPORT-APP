Actual staff comparison projects monthly cost using the entered headcount and payroll assumptions. It does not represent recorded payroll expenses.

How it works

  1. Daily sales = sales so far divided by days open.
  2. Expected monthly sales = daily sales multiplied by open days this month.
  3. Recommended staff = daily sales divided by sales per staff, rounded up.
  4. Monthly labor cost = staff multiplied by daily pay and salary days, plus other costs.
  5. Labor percentage = monthly labor cost divided by expected monthly sales, multiplied by 100.

About these estimates

The overall percentage uses combined labor costs divided by combined sales; it is not the average of branch percentages.

The total labor budget adds up each branch’s allowed cost. The overall percentage uses total costs and total sales. Salary days can differ from days open. Add estimated employer contributions, benefits, overtime and premiums under “Other costs” to include them in the projection.

Staff recommendations are a sales-based planning guide. Review shift coverage, workload and rest days before deployment.
