🛒 Puregold & Supplier Price Catalog

Search a product and select Add to PR. Product, pack/UOM and unit price fill automatically; enter quantity and the PO total updates. Prefer PER PC when each item is sold individually or the number of pieces per pack is verified. Otherwise use PER PACK. Prices are estimates until verified against the latest receipt. Catalog changes are saved in this browser; export a backup when sharing with other branches.

CSV columns: Item, UOM, Price, Supplier, Barcode, Notes, PiecesPerPack, PackPrice, PieceUOM. Price is the original selling-unit price; PiecesPerPack plus PackPrice lets the app calculate per-piece cost. Open an Excel sheet and save it as CSV UTF-8 to upload. The list is separate from your actual saved PR/PO records.
