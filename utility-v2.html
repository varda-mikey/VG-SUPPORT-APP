Use this when there is no meter reading and billing is based on the tenant’s equipment. Monthly kWh = Watts × Quantity × Hours/Day × Usage Factor × Days Open ÷ 1,000.
Usage Factor Guide
1.00 — Continuous: running for the full hours entered.
0.80 — Heavy: running most of the time.
0.60 — Frequent: used regularly during the day.
0.40 — Moderate: cycles on and off, like some chillers/freezers.
0.25 — Light: used only occasionally.
Use the factor that best matches actual operation.
