48-hour planner

1. Click Plan week beside a staff name.
2. Choose 8, 9, 10 or 11 hours and a start time for each day.
3. Review the total, then Apply week.
Example: 2 days × 9h + 3 days × 10h = 48h. No extra minutes are added.
